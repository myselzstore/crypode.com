Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Crypode, Muscat, Oman

Short version: We don't sell your data, we don't track you with advertising cookies, and we only collect your email if you choose to subscribe to our newsletter.

Who We Are

Crypode (crypode.com) is a personal finance publication based in Muscat, Oman, focused on halal investing and financial guidance for Gulf professionals. The site is run by Fahad Al-Rawahi.

For privacy questions, contact us at: hello@crypode.com

What We Collect

We collect the minimum necessary to operate the site:

  • Email address — only if you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter via the email box on the homepage. We use this solely to notify you of new articles and updates from Crypode.
  • Anonymous usage data — via Google Analytics (GA4), which collects aggregate browsing data such as page views and approximate location (country/city level). No personal identifiers are stored by Google Analytics.

We do not collect names, payment information, or any other personal data.

Email Subscriptions

When you enter your email address in our newsletter form:

  • Your email is saved to a private Google Sheet accessible only to us.
  • You are redirected to our Medium publication to follow us there.
  • We will never share or sell your email address to third parties.
  • You can unsubscribe at any time by emailing hello@crypode.com and we will remove your address within 48 hours.

Cookies

This site uses a small number of cookies and local storage entries:

  • Google Analytics cookies — these are third-party cookies set by Google to measure aggregate site traffic. They do not identify you personally. See Google's cookie policy for details.
  • Cookie preference — a local storage entry (crypode_privacy_ok) that remembers you have accepted this notice, so the banner doesn't appear on every visit. No data leaves your browser from this entry.

We do not use advertising cookies, retargeting cookies, or any cookies for commercial tracking.

Affiliate Links

Some links on this site are affiliate links — if you click one and sign up for a service, we may earn a small commission at no additional cost to you. These links are always labelled with rel="sponsored" or identified clearly in the content. When you click an affiliate link, we log the click (affiliate name, page, and timestamp) to our private analytics sheet to understand which content is helpful. No personal data is recorded for affiliate clicks.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics — analytics. Privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — fonts loaded from Google's CDN. Privacy policy.
  • Medium — our newsletter redirects to Medium's subscription page. Privacy policy.
  • Spotify — our podcast is hosted on Spotify. Privacy policy.
  • Affiliate networks (Impact.com, ShareASale, etc.) — may set their own cookies when you click affiliate links. We have no control over their data practices.

Data Retention

Email addresses collected via newsletter subscription are retained until you request deletion. Anonymous Google Analytics data is retained according to Google's standard 14-month retention policy. Affiliate click logs contain no personal data and are retained indefinitely for our internal analytics.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your email from our subscribers list.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics by using Google's opt-out browser add-on.
  • Ask what data we hold about you.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@crypode.com.

Children's Privacy

Crypode is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
