"I built Crypode because no one was writing honestly about Gulf money — the debt, the family pressure, the faith questions."

Fahad Al Rawahi is a Gulf aviation professional, father of six, and the founder of Crypode. Based in Muscat, Oman, he spent years navigating personal debt, halal finance questions and the pressures of being a sole provider — without finding a single resource that spoke honestly to that reality.

Crypode is the resource he wished had existed. Every article, tool and podcast episode is written from lived experience — not generic financial theory.

All content is educational and does not constitute regulated financial advice.
